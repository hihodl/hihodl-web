# Spaces homepage story: the six images

The hihodl.xyz homepage tells one story in six full-screen images: a creator arrives at a conference with a suitcase covered in brand stickers, people stop, she films, she interviews, and that evening the brand writes back. Each image carries one line of text. The images do the selling.

## How to deliver them

- Put each file in `public/spaces/story/` with the exact file name below. The page picks it up by itself. Until a file is there, that scene shows its drawing instead.
- **Desktop format: 16:9, 2560 × 1440 or larger** (generate at 2K and upscale to 4K if you can). JPG at quality 80 to 85, under 1 MB. Next resizes it for each screen.
- **Phone format (optional but recommended for 01 and 04): 9:16, 1440 × 2560**, saved as `<name>-mobile.jpg`, for example `01-arrival-mobile.jpg`. On phones it replaces the 16:9 image. Without it, phones show the right part of the 16:9 image.
- **Composition rule for all six:** the subject sits in the **right half** of the frame. The **bottom left third stays quiet** (floor, blurred crowd, dark wall), because that is where the text goes over a dark gradient.
- **Same woman and same suitcase in every image.** Generate 01 first. Then use it as the character and suitcase reference (Soul ID or image reference) for 02 to 06.

## The shared description (paste into every prompt)

> **Character:** a woman in her late twenties, a confident content creator, warm olive skin, dark wavy shoulder-length hair, oversized cream linen blazer over a black t-shirt, black wide-leg trousers, white sneakers, a small black crossbody bag, a conference lanyard.
>
> **Suitcase:** a cream hard-shell cabin suitcase whose front is covered in a neat grid of about twelve rectangular brand stickers in different bright colours (amber, cobalt blue, mint, coral, black, white), each a simple abstract symbol with no readable words, plus a large sticker across the top.
>
> **Setting:** a large modern technology conference in Singapore, glass and steel atrium, tropical plants, crowds with lanyards.
>
> **Style:** cinematic editorial photography, 35 mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, warm amber highlights and deep teal and navy shadows, soft film grain, photorealistic.
>
> **Avoid:** readable text, real brand logos, event logos, watermarks, distorted hands or faces, extra fingers, cartoon look.

The stickers must never show real brands or the event's logo. The event page states that HOLD is not affiliated with the event, and an image must not suggest otherwise.

## Check before shipping

1. Look at every sticker at 100% zoom. There must be no real logos and no letters that read as a word.
2. Check her hands and the microphone and gimbal grip.
3. Check that the bottom left of each image is dark enough to read white text over it. The page adds a gradient, but a bright floor still fights it.
4. Keep the same woman, the same blazer and the same suitcase in all six.
